Projects · Timeline

Timeline

Thinkers, works, events and concepts on one band of years, every node cited. The page is being built today from the mock-up; the guide says what it will show. Guide
collect

Wikidata, by written rules

Every person with a Stanford or Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry, one hop of influence, their works, the wars, revolutions, treaties and periods with enough coverage, the concepts and movements.

Encyclopedia entries and Wikipedia leads

Title, authors and revision of each Stanford entry for its citation; each node's extract with its revision id; link-outs to Britannica, ODNB, Oxford Reference, Gutenberg, the Internet Archive.
connect

A model proposes thinker-to-event connections

From the extract only, quoting the sentence that supports each; checked verbatim; a proposal until accepted in the page.

Layers by what connects (thinkers to events, works to works…) and by whose connections; decisions and notes to log.jsonl.

In progress, 19 November 2026: the Wikidata collection is running (about 9,000 nodes); the page follows from Ben's 2020s mock-up. The node set and the connection types are written rules, open to change.

Every footnote and every bibliography entry

From Word's footnotes.xml or LaTeX's \footnote and .bib, with chapter and position.

Nine named rules

Numbering gaps, broken ibid. chains, page-range dashes, punctuation, quotation marks, short forms with no entry, first citations not in full, entries never cited, one work under several names. A short form is resolved only to exactly one entry; otherwise it is a question for Ben.

Sees candidate entries from his own bibliography; its answer is a proposal in review.csv.

The shape of the work

diverge

Many readers, many candidates, many proposals

OCR engines and vision models on a page; gazetteers on a name; Wikidata and a model on a thinker; rules on a footnote.
converge

Consensus, or a written rule

Agreement measured, every disagreement marked and weighted; a rule with a reason where one can be written.
centre

A person decides

Every project ends in a page where Ben settles what the machinery could not: a passage, a translation, a connection, a tag, a footnote. The decision is an event in a log, never a silent edit.
record

Provenance on everything

A line knows its reader and prompt; a node its rule and identifiers; a connection its statement, model or author; a change its decision.

Where the evidence is

questionfile

What did a person decide, ask or copy, and when?outputs/reading/log.jsonl and outputs/timeline/log.jsonl; the text decisions produced in outputs/reading/builds/<build_id>/
Why is this thinker connected to this event?edges in outputs/timeline/timeline.sqlite: the Wikidata property, or the model, its reason and the sentence it quoted; the rule that admitted each node in inputs/rules.json
What did Wikidata, the Stanford Encyclopedia or Wikipedia actually answer?cache/wikidata/, cache/sep/, cache/wikipedia/ (request and raw reply)
Where do the readers disagree, and how much should I trust each?outputs/consensus/consensus.json, checkin.csv, and the consensus page
What did the gazetteer actually answer?cache/gazetteer_<source>/<hash>.json (request and raw reply)
Why is this dot here?operations/work/decisions.json, and the panel on the map
What is still a person's call?operations/work/review.csv, column your_choice; proposals with status proposed in the timeline
