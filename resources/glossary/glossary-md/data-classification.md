# Data Classification

> **In one line:** Harvard sorts data by sensitivity level; on the temporary HUIT
> plan used in this workshop you may only put lower-sensitivity material (up to
> Level 2) into Claude until the Anthropic Enterprise agreement is in place.

## In plain terms

Harvard classifies information by how sensitive it is. The accounts you're using
today are a **temporary HUIT-provided bridge** — used for that
workshop — and they do **not** yet carry the Harvard–[Anthropic](anthropic.md)
Enterprise agreement that governs sensitive data.

What that means in practice:

- For today, use only material you'd be comfortable sharing — **nothing above
  Harvard Level 2.** Student work and research data wait for the agreement.
- The temporary account and Enterprise agreement have different data-handling
  terms. Record the account and agreement used for a project alongside its
  data sources.
- Once Harvard rolls out the full Enterprise plan, the rules change — that's the
  point at which higher-sensitivity work becomes appropriate.

This intersects with two features you'll turn on: [memory](memory.md) (Claude
saving notes about your work) and [Cowork](claude-cowork.md) (Claude reading a
folder on your machine). Memory retains information across sessions; Cowork reads the folder you
select. A dedicated project folder makes that scope explicit.

## Why it matters in this workshop

This glossary was adapted from the desktop-app workshop. That setup used
temporary HUIT accounts with a Level 2 ceiling. Record the account arrangement
for the session you are running rather than treating that setup as universal.
