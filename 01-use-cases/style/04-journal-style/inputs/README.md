# Inputs

Never committed: `samples/` (three or more recent articles from the
journal, as PDF with a text layer, Word or plain text) and `draft/` (your
article as `.docx`). Nothing here is modified by any command.

`config/` is committed: one profile per journal, written by
`journal profile` and edited by hand where a decision was left empty. A
profile quotes short excerpts from the journal's articles as its examples.
`fixtures/` is committed: a synthetic draft and three synthetic articles.
