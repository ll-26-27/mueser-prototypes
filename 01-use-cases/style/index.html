Style & documents

Make recurring document work easier while keeping your own form: the handout, the CV, the chapter and its notes.

These are scoped workflows, not working document tools yet. The next step is to test each one against a small set of your documents.

Handouts

Infer a house style from existing examples, apply it without changing the words, and update course details for a new term.

Read the brief →

CV

Add entries by copying the formatting of neighbouring entries, then report changes to page count and layout.

Read the brief →

Footnotes & bibliography

Find inconsistent citations and unmatched short forms. Review proposed fixes before applying them to a copy.

Read the brief →

What you bring

Three to five handouts, your current CV, and a chapter or manuscript. Include the relevant citation rules, bibliography and examples of the form you want.

What comes back

The planned output is a new document plus a report of proposed or applied changes. Source documents remain available for comparison.

A document, a rule, a reviewable change
