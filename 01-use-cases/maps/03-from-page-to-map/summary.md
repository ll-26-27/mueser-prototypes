# From page to map: summary

**Purpose.** Show the whole chain the two halves were built for: a page
image → a machine transcription → mentions with line numbers → records →
gazetteer resolution → a map that links back to the line.

**Workflow.** Kraken's OCR of five synthetic pages → a mention list made in
session and recorded as such → verbatim check → 88 records → resolver → map.

**Findings.** 36 of 56 distinct place names resolved automatically, 52 of 73
place mentions are on the map, every one of them traceable to a page and
line. OCR errors in names ("Verlin") pass through because the record keeps
both the garbled surface form and a normalised guess. Rivers, regions,
buildings and city quarters are what the gazetteers cannot settle from a
name alone.

**Limits.** The extraction was done by a model in session, not through the
API; the comparison of the two is the next check. Five synthetic pages.
