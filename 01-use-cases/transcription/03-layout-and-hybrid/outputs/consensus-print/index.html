Hybrid reading, print: consensus and check-in

No ground truth is needed for this page. Every reading of a page is compared with every other; the medoid is the reading closest to all the rest and is shown as the base text, in the declared normalisation (ſ folded to s, so that is not counted as a disagreement). Every span where at least one other reader differs is marked; darker means more readers disagree. Hover a mark for each reader's alternative. Spans where the dissenting readers carry at least 25% of the page's total reliability (a reader's reliability is its mean agreement with the others, so four weak OCR models dissenting together count for less than one strong model) are the check-in list (checkin.csv) for a person to settle; where ground truth exists the medoid's own error rate is shown, so you can see whether agreement predicted correctness.

Readers are grouped into the OCR family (Tesseract, Kraken, eScriptorium: segment, then recognise, no language sense beyond their training) and the vision family (vision-language models and the hybrid mode: read the image as a whole, with a language sense that can both fix and invent). The family gap is the distance between the two families' medoids.
