# CLAUDE.md — Kurrent handwriting

Read the repository `CLAUDE.md` first. In this folder the rules of the
Fraktur use case apply, and two more:

- The real pages are handwriting with community transcriptions, not
  editions. Do not "correct" a ground-truth file toward what a model read.
- A reading of a famous hand may come from memory (Schnitzler is in training
  data). The prompt forbids it; when you see a suspiciously fluent reading of
  a smudged line, say so in the report.
