# Synthetic source text

`briefe.txt` holds three **made-up** documents written for this repository on
4 November 2026 by Claude (Fable 5.1): two letters and a page of household
accounts, all set in the summer of 1786 and sharing the places, dates and
people of the synthetic travel journal in
`01-fraktur-print-bakeoff/inputs/synthetic-text/`. None of it is a historical
document; the people are invented, the places are real.

The pages are rendered in **Wiegel Kurrent** (Peter Wiegel, SIL Open Font
License), a typeface that imitates Kurrent letterforms. A font is not
handwriting: letters are identical every time, spacing is regular, there are
no ligatures of haste, no corrections, no bleed-through. Treat the synthetic
pages as a check that a backend can read Kurrent letterforms at all, and the
three real pages in `inputs/pages/real-*` as the test that matters.
