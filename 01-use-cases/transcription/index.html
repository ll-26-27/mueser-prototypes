Working example

Reading room

Read an old German source beside its scan and translation. Inspect disagreements, settle passages and take a quotation with its citation.

Open the reading room →How the workspace works

What you bring

Page scans or photographs, plus the title, author, date and archive details needed to cite them. The current example uses three historical pages: print, handwriting and Antiqua type.

What you get

A scan beside a starting transcription and English translation; marked disagreements; a record of your choices; and passages you can copy with citations.

From a scan to a reading you can cite

The working example is built. Bringing in your own scans still uses the project commands; there is no upload-and-process button yet.

Workflow diagram · available offline
  1. The desktop agent or a terminal runs the reading pipeline on your scans. Multiple readers supply alternatives; agreement provides a starting point, not a guarantee of correctness.
  2. The page brings the scan, text, translation and evidence together. You decide the passages that need judgment.
  3. The local server logs your actions. Applying decisions writes a new build, keeping the original diplomatic reading alongside it.
