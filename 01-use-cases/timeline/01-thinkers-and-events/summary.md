# Summary

**Purpose.** A page for students and for Ben where a thinker, a work, an event, a period or a concept sits on the band of its years, with what came before it, what it touched, and every reference a reader would need to go further, each one a link to a real entry. The design is Ben's own mock-up from some years ago; the data is drawn by written rules rather than typed in, so it can grow.

**Workflow.** `collect` reads Wikidata by the rules in `inputs/rules.json`: every person with a Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy or Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry, the people one hop of influence away above a coverage threshold, their works, the wars, revolutions, treaties and periods above a threshold, the concepts with an encyclopedia entry and the movements people belong to; and every statement between two such items becomes a recorded connection with its property named. `sources` fetches each Stanford entry's own citation metadata and each node's Wikipedia lead with its revision. `connect` gives a model one person's extract and the events overlapping that person's life, and asks which bear on the person, with a type from a closed list and the sentence of the extract that supports each; the sentence is checked verbatim. `db` puts nodes, identifiers, links, texts, citations and connections into one SQLite database and one JSON file; `build` renders the page; `serve` saves every decision, note and excerpt to an append-only log.

**Findings.** The counts of nodes by type and rule, of connections by source and standing, and of proposals whose evidence was found verbatim are in the README, with the run ids they come from. The Stanford Encyclopedia's own metadata gives a complete citation for every entry it covers; Wikidata's external identifiers reach Britannica, the ODNB, Oxford Reference and the text archives for most people and works of the seed, more thinly for events.

**Limits.** The node set is only as wide as the two encyclopedias and one hop of Wikidata's influence statements; a thinker without an entry and without a recorded influence is absent until the rule is widened. Dates carry Wikidata's precision and gaps. The overview is Wikipedia's lead, attributed, not a text of this repository. The model's proposals rest on the lead section alone, so they are few and conservative by design; a connection that needs a book to establish it belongs to a later perspective, read from that book with its page. The images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons when the page is online.
