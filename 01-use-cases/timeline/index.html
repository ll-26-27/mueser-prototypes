Timeline

Explore thinkers, works, events and concepts together. Follow the connections, inspect their sources, and add your own interpretations.

The viewer and pipeline are implemented and tested with synthetic data. The first real-data page is still being assembled; this overview is available while that run finishes.

What goes in

A written rule for the people, works, events and concepts to include. Wikidata supplies records and identifiers; encyclopedia metadata and Wikipedia extracts provide references and context.

What you get

A timeline and connection map, with links and citations. Recorded relationships, model proposals and connections you add remain distinguishable.

From reference records to a reviewed connection

A model’s proposed connection is shown as a proposal. A recorded connection retains its source property; your own connections carry your name.

Workflow diagram · available offline
  1. The desktop agent or a terminal collects records under the written rules and caches the service replies.
  2. A task model proposes connections from the supplied extracts. Its quoted evidence is checked against the text; proposals still need a reader’s judgment.
  3. You explore and decide in the browser. The local server saves decisions and notes for the next build.
