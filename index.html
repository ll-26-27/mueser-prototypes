One journey through all seven

The journal pages were rendered from an invented text whose places are the ledger's rows; Kraken's reading of those pages is the bridge's input; the overlay search runs on the points the ledger resolved; the map-data case joins its region to the same route; the real pages of the two bake-offs are the layout case's inputs. Follow "Breßlau" from a Fraktur line in the first explorer to a GOV record in the maps.
